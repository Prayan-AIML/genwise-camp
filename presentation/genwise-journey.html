Project 1 · for young children

🐉 Mumble — a buddy that learns to speak with you

A speech-practice app for kids with speech delays. The child talks, a gentle A.I. coach replies out loud, and a little creature grows as they practise — so the child is the teacher, never the patient.

Project 2 · a science experiment

🎲 Which A.I. plays Ludo the smartest?

Ludo is mostly luck — so "who wins" just measures the dice. Instead, I gave several A.I.s the exact same board and scored how good each one's move was, compared to an expert. That measures real thinking, not lucky rolls.
